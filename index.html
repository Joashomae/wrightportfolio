02 / APPROACH

Observe carefully.
Reason clearly.
Check the result.

A strong technical answer starts with what the evidence actually supports. My approach separates visible details from assumptions and makes each conclusion traceable.

  1. Identify the evidence

    Isolate the relevant features in the image, diagram, or technical document.

  2. Frame the engineering question

    Define a realistic decision with meaningful constraints and a checkable outcome.

  3. Build the reference reasoning

    State assumptions, choose suitable equations, show calculations, and verify units.

  4. Evaluate consistently

    Use clear scoring criteria, distinguish partial credit from critical errors, and resolve ambiguity.
